# 商务中文核心词表 · Business Chinese Core Vocabulary

A single-file, dependency-free reference of **284 essential Business Chinese terms** across **10 domains** — with 汉字, 拼音 and English glosses.

**Open it here:** [index.html](index.html) ｜ or download and double-click — no build step, no server, no internet required.

**Lesson plan:** [lesson-1-first-meeting.html](lesson-1-first-meeting.html) — Business Chinese, Lesson 1.

---

## What it is

A practical vocabulary list for learners who need Chinese for work: banking, payments, credit, foreign exchange, securities, funds, insurance, corporate finance, regulation and general business.

Built for adult learners and professionals. Every entry is a term you will actually meet in Chinese business documents, contracts, statements and meetings — not textbook filler.

## Features

- **284 terms / 10 domains** — filter by domain with one click
- **Search** across Chinese, pinyin and English at the same time
- **Two modes** — a plain table for study, and a flip-card mode for drilling
- **Print-friendly** — the print stylesheet strips the UI and lays out a clean word list
- **Single file, zero dependencies** — all data is embedded; it works offline and from a USB stick
- **Light and dark** — follows your system theme

## Lesson plan

[**Lesson 1 · First Meeting**](lesson-1-first-meeting.html) — a 60-minute lesson structure for adult beginners: how to address someone, exchange name cards and take leave. Five target sentences with pinyin, three ways to address a colleague, and a five-stage timing plan. Single file, zero dependencies.

## How to use it

1. Open `index.html` in any browser (or use the link above).
2. Type in the search box to look up a term — it matches 汉字, pinyin and English.
3. Click a domain chip to narrow the list.
4. Switch to **词卡 / Card** mode and click a card to reveal pinyin and meaning. Use ← → to move, space to flip.
5. Hit **打印 / Print** for a paper copy.
